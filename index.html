About PostClip

PostClip is a free, browser-based clipping tool for YouTube streamers. Record a live stream and mark the best moments as they happen, cut clips from a recorded video file, or pick clip points from a YouTube VOD link — then export in 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9, ready for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. Everything runs locally in your browser; no video is ever uploaded to a server.
